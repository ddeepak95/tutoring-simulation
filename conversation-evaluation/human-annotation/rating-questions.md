# Initial Teaching Response

### Topic Introduction and Learner Engagement

**Question:** How effectively does the chatbot introduce the topic, spark the learner's interest, and explore their prior knowledge and learning goals?

**Additional guidance:** Evaluate how effectively the chatbot establishes an engaging entry point into the topic, stimulates curiosity, and meaningfully explores the learner's existing knowledge, interests, or learning goals. Consider whether it uses age-appropriate hooks (e.g., intriguing questions, relatable scenarios, surprising facts, or real-world relevance) and purposeful questions to understand the learner's starting point. Higher scores should reflect authentic curiosity-building, meaningful learner exploration, and responsive personalization rather than generic greetings or formulaic questions.

- **5 — Exceptional:** Opens with a particularly compelling, creative, and age-appropriate hook that sparks genuine curiosity and makes the topic immediately interesting or relevant. Thoughtfully explores the learner's prior knowledge, interests, or learning goals through specific, purposeful questions or conversational moves, and meaningfully uses available learner information to tailor the introduction and direction of learning.
- **4 — Effective:** Introduces the topic clearly and engagingly using a relevant hook or inviting opening. Makes a meaningful attempt to explore the learner's prior knowledge, interests, or learning goals and shows some responsiveness to their needs, though the engagement or personalization lacks the depth and creativity of an exceptional introduction.
- **3 — Adequate:** Provides a clear introduction that orients the learner to the topic and offers a basic opportunity for participation. May include a generic question or simple attempt to spark interest or explore prior knowledge, but engagement and personalization remain limited or superficial.
- **2 — Weak:** Provides a perfunctory, generic, or minimally engaging introduction, with little effort to spark curiosity or understand the learner's starting point. Any questions about prior knowledge or learning goals feel formulaic, disconnected, or are not meaningfully used to guide the interaction.
- **1 — Ineffective:** Fails to meaningfully introduce or orient the learner to the topic, immediately launches into an unfocused explanation, or disregards explicitly stated interests, prior knowledge, or learning goals. Provides little to no opportunity for engagement or learner participation.

### Amount of Information

**Question:** How appropriately does the chatbot adjust the amount and depth of information to a high-school learner's needs?

**Additional guidance:** Evaluate how effectively the chatbot calibrates the quantity, depth, and pacing of information to the learner's age, prior knowledge, expressed needs, and current stage of learning. Consider whether it prioritizes essential concepts, introduces supporting details at appropriate moments, and avoids unnecessary complexity, repetition, or cognitive overload. More information is not inherently better; higher scores should reflect increasingly precise, purposeful, and adaptive information delivery rather than simply greater detail or brevity.

- **5 — Exceptional:** Demonstrates exceptional precision in selecting, prioritizing, and sequencing information to match the learner's needs and current understanding. Strategically introduces essential ideas and supporting details at just the right depth and pace, making complex material manageable without oversimplification. Anticipates when additional explanation is necessary and when to avoid unnecessary detail, achieving an exceptionally effective balance of completeness, clarity, and cognitive load.
- **4 — Effective:** Provides a well-paced, appropriately detailed explanation that covers essential information without significant gaps or unnecessary complexity. Generally matches the learner's level and needs, with only minor excess, repetition, omissions, or opportunities for more precise calibration.
- **3 — Adequate:** Provides sufficient information to understand the main idea, but the amount, depth, or pacing is only generally appropriate. Some parts may be overly detailed, too brief, repetitive, or unevenly developed, though these issues do not substantially prevent understanding.
- **2 — Weak:** Provides noticeably too much or too little information for the learner's needs. Explanations may be overly dense, excessively simplified, repetitive, or digressive, creating unnecessary cognitive load or leaving important aspects insufficiently developed.
- **1 — Ineffective:** Severely misjudges the amount or depth of information, either omitting essential content or overwhelming the learner with excessive detail, complexity, or irrelevant material. The resulting explanation is substantially inaccessible or insufficient for meaningful understanding.

### Naturalness of the Conversation

**Question:** How natural, approachable, and conversational is the chatbot's response?

**Additional guidance:** Evaluate how closely the chatbot resembles a natural conversation with a friendly teacher or peer. Consider its use of conversational moves (e.g., discourse markers, backchanneling, interactional markers, acknowledgments, and follow-up questions), responsiveness to the student's contributions, warmth, age-appropriate language, and overall conversational flow. Higher scores should reflect authentic, engaging dialogue rather than simply clear or friendly explanations.

- **5 — Exceptional:** Feels like a genuine conversation with a friendly teacher or peer, naturally incorporating a rich variety of conversational moves. Responds thoughtfully to the student's wording and ideas, creating a warm, engaging, dynamic, and authentic back-and-forth.
- **4 — Effective:** Sounds friendly, approachable, and conversational throughout. Responds naturally to the student's input and maintains a comfortable flow, though it uses relatively few conversational moves or lacks the spontaneity and richness of a genuine conversation.
- **3 — Adequate:** Demonstrates basic conversational qualities and is generally comfortable to interact with. Uses some friendly or conversational language, but responses may feel generic, somewhat formal, or mildly awkward at times. The exchange is understandable and approachable but lacks a consistently natural conversational flow.
- **2 — Weak:** Sounds more like a textbook or scripted tutor than a conversational partner. Relies heavily on formal explanations, rigid structures, or formulaic conversational phrases (e.g., repeatedly saying "Great question!" or "Let's explore this"). Attempts at conversation feel mechanical, repetitive, or disconnected from the student's contributions.
- **1 — Ineffective:** Does not resemble a natural conversation. Responses are robotic, impersonal, overly formal, or entirely instructional, with little to no meaningful acknowledgment of the student's input. The chatbot primarily delivers information rather than engaging in dialogue, making the interaction feel unnatural or discouraging.

### Examples, Analogies, and Real-World Connections

**Question:** How effectively does the chatbot use explanatory devices (such as examples, analogies, or real-world connections) to explain the concept?

**Additional guidance:** Evaluate the relevance, accuracy, variety, and explanatory value of the devices used to make concepts more understandable. Consider whether the chatbot clearly connects each device to the underlying concept rather than merely mentioning it. Higher scores should reflect purposeful use of complementary devices that illuminate different aspects of the concept, rather than simply a greater number of examples.

- **5 — Exceptional:** Skillfully integrates several well-chosen, accurate, and complementary explanatory devices (e.g., examples, analogies, and real-world connections) that illuminate different aspects of the concept. Clearly explains how these devices relate to the underlying idea, making abstract or difficult concepts intuitive, concrete, and applicable in new contexts.
- **4 — Effective:** Uses one or two well-chosen, accurate, and relevant explanatory devices that clearly illustrate the concept. Explains their connection to the underlying idea sufficiently to support understanding, though additional variety or depth could further enrich the explanation.
- **3 — Adequate:** Uses at least one relevant explanatory device that conveys the main point, but its connection to the concept is briefly explained, underdeveloped, or only partially unpacked. Supports basic understanding without substantially deepening it.
- **2 — Weak:** Uses an underdeveloped, generic, or loosely related explanatory device that adds little clarity, or omits one when the learner would likely benefit from it. Connections to the underlying concept are unclear or insufficiently explained.
- **1 — Ineffective:** Uses inaccurate, misleading, or confusing explanatory devices that distort the concept, or fails to provide a needed explanatory device for a difficult concept, leaving the explanation substantially unclear.

### Memory Aids and Key Takeaways

**Question:** How effectively does the chatbot use memory aids such as mnemonics, key takeaways, or summaries to help the learner remember the concept?

**Additional guidance:** Evaluate the chatbot's use of deliberate memory-support strategies that help learners retain and retrieve key concepts. These may include dedicated summaries, key takeaways, mnemonics, analogies designed for recall, retrieval cues, or practical tips for remembering. Simply organizing information into bullet points or presenting a clear explanation does not constitute a memory aid. Higher scores should reflect increasingly purposeful, memorable, and effective reinforcement beyond basic summarization.

- **5 — Exceptional:** Goes beyond a standard summary by providing exceptionally clear, concise, and memorable reinforcement through dedicated key takeaways and well-designed memory aids (e.g., meaningful mnemonics, retrieval cues, or other established recall strategies). Distills the central concept into an easily retrievable form and helps the learner remember and apply it later.
- **4 — Effective:** Provides a clear, accurate, and dedicated summary or key takeaway, along with useful tips or cues for recalling the central concept. Reinforcement is purposeful and supports retention, though the memory aids may be relatively conventional or less distinctive.
- **3 — Adequate:** Provides a basic recap, takeaway, or simple recall cue that correctly reinforces the main concept, but it is generic, incomplete, or not particularly memorable. Offers some support for retention without employing especially effective or deliberate memory strategies.
- **2 — Weak:** Makes limited or superficial attempts at reinforcement, such as vague takeaways, repetitive restatements, or overly lengthy summaries. Memory aids are poorly designed, weakly connected to the concept, or missing when clearly needed. Formatting information into bullet points may substitute for meaningful recall support.
- **1 — Ineffective:** Provides no meaningful summary, takeaway, or memory aid despite a clear need for reinforcement, or offers misleading, inaccurate, or confusing recall cues that undermine the learner's ability to remember the concept.

### Checking Understanding

**Question:** How effectively does the chatbot check the learner's understanding of the concept?

**Additional guidance:** Evaluate how effectively the chatbot uses questions or brief tasks to determine what the learner currently understands. Focus on the relevance, diagnostic value, and appropriateness of these checks, including whether they reveal reasoning or potential misconceptions. The number of questions should suit the context; neither more nor fewer questions automatically indicate better assessment. Evaluate recommendations for future practice or learning activities under Guidance for Next Steps instead.

- **5 — Exceptional:** Uses exceptionally insightful, targeted questions or tasks that probe the learner's conceptual understanding, reveal underlying reasoning or potential misconceptions, and provide rich evidence of what the learner has grasped. The checks are thoughtfully designed and appropriately calibrated to the learner and context.
- **4 — Effective:** Uses relevant, purposeful questions or tasks that meaningfully assess understanding of the central concept. Provides useful evidence of learning, though the checks could reveal deeper reasoning or misconceptions more effectively.
- **3 — Adequate:** Provides a relevant but basic check of understanding, primarily assessing recall or straightforward application. Offers some evidence of learning but limited insight into deeper understanding.
- **2 — Weak:** Uses generic, superficial, poorly targeted, or unnecessarily repetitive questions that provide little meaningful evidence of understanding, or misses an important opportunity to check comprehension.
- **1 — Ineffective:** Provides no meaningful check when one is clearly needed, or uses irrelevant, misleading, or fundamentally inappropriate questions that fail to assess understanding.

### Language Alignment in Learning Materials

**Question:** How effectively does the chatbot use the language of the conversation to support learning across explanations, examples, and visual materials?

**Additional guidance:** Evaluate how consistently and effectively the chatbot uses the language of the conversation across all instructional content, including written explanations, examples, diagrams, charts, tables, labels, captions, and other visuals. Consider whether terminology and expressions are natural, accessible, and appropriate for the learner, rather than unnecessarily relying on foreign-language terms or materials. Foreign-language terminology is acceptable when it is commonly used, technically necessary, or educationally useful, particularly when appropriately explained. Higher scores should reflect thoughtful linguistic adaptation that actively supports understanding, not simply the absence of foreign words.

- **5 — Exceptional:** Skillfully leverages the language of the conversation across all relevant instructional content, including explanations and visuals, to make learning especially intuitive and accessible. Uses precise, natural, and learner-appropriate terminology, thoughtfully adapting labels, examples, and other materials to the learner's linguistic context. Any foreign-language elements are purposefully included and clearly contextualized to enhance understanding.
- **4 — Effective:** Consistently uses the language of the conversation across explanations and relevant instructional materials, with clear and appropriate terminology. Foreign-language elements are used only when justified and are adequately explained, with minor opportunities for stronger linguistic adaptation.
- **3 — Adequate:** Primarily uses the language of the conversation and communicates the main concepts, but some terminology, visual labels, or supporting materials remain in another language or use less natural expressions, creating minor barriers to understanding.
- **2 — Weak:** Frequently relies on unnecessary foreign-language terminology, untranslated visual labels, or poorly adapted instructional materials when accessible alternatives exist. These inconsistencies make learning noticeably more difficult.
- **1 — Ineffective:** Substantially depends on unexplained foreign-language content across explanations or instructional materials, creating major linguistic barriers that prevent the learner from meaningfully understanding the concepts.

### Active Learning and Learner Participation

**Question:** How effectively does the chatbot encourage the learner to actively engage with and construct understanding of the concept rather than passively receive information?

**Additional guidance:** Evaluate how effectively the chatbot creates meaningful opportunities for active learning through reasoning, exploration, prediction, problem-solving, reflection, or guided discovery. Consider whether the learner is encouraged to think through ideas, make connections, and participate in developing understanding rather than simply receiving explanations. Active learning does not necessarily require multiple questions or elaborate activities; a well-placed prompt or task can be sufficient. Focus on participation in the learning process rather than assessment of understanding or recommendations for future learning.

- **5 — Exceptional:** Skillfully integrates highly purposeful and engaging active-learning opportunities that enable the learner to explore, reason through, or discover important ideas. Uses thoughtfully designed prompts, tasks, or guided interactions that give the learner a meaningful role in constructing understanding, rather than simply receiving information.
- **4 — Effective:** Provides relevant and meaningful opportunities for the learner to actively engage with the concept through reasoning, exploration, problem-solving, or reflection. Encourages participation beyond passive listening, though the activities may be relatively straightforward or less deeply integrated into the explanation.
- **3 — Adequate:** Includes a basic opportunity for active participation, such as a simple prediction, reflection, or guided reasoning prompt, but the interaction is limited or only loosely integrated into the learning process. Most understanding is still developed through direct explanation.
- **2 — Weak:** Relies predominantly on passive information delivery, with only superficial or poorly connected attempts to involve the learner. Opportunities for meaningful reasoning, exploration, or participation are limited or missed.
- **1 — Ineffective:** Provides an entirely passive learning experience when active engagement is clearly warranted, or discourages the learner's participation by consistently supplying answers without allowing opportunities to think, explore, or contribute.

### Addressing Follow-Up Questions

**Question:** How effectively does the chatbot respond to the learner's follow-up question while using the context of the earlier conversation?

**Additional guidance:** Evaluate how accurately and directly the chatbot addresses the learner's follow-up question while maintaining continuity with the preceding conversation. Consider whether it recognizes the learner's specific intent or confusion, uses relevant prior context, and adapts its response rather than unnecessarily repeating earlier explanations. Higher scores should reflect increasingly thoughtful and context-sensitive responses, not simply greater detail or length. Focus on responsiveness and conversational continuity rather than general explanation quality.

- **5 — Exceptional:** Demonstrates a particularly insightful understanding of the learner's follow-up question and underlying need. Seamlessly integrates relevant earlier context, directly addresses the specific confusion or curiosity, and thoughtfully adapts the response to advance the learner's understanding without unnecessary repetition.
- **4 — Effective:** Directly and accurately answers the follow-up question, appropriately drawing on relevant earlier context. Shows clear responsiveness to the learner's needs, though minor opportunities for deeper contextual tailoring remain.
- **3 — Adequate:** Sufficiently answers the main follow-up question but makes limited use of earlier context or provides a somewhat generic response. May leave minor aspects of the learner's question or confusion insufficiently addressed.
- **2 — Weak:** Partially addresses the follow-up but overlooks an important aspect of the learner's question, makes insufficient use of relevant context, or repeats earlier information without meaningfully adapting the explanation.
- **1 — Ineffective:** Fails to address the learner's follow-up question, ignores essential conversational context, or provides a substantially irrelevant, confusing, or incorrect response.
