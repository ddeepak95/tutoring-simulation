# Instructional item labeling guide

Label tutor sections using the items below. Student turns supply context only. Categories are fixed metadata; annotators select items. Each section can have several different items. Save one annotation per section/item pair with an occurrence count from 1 to 1,000 (default 1). Count repeated occurrences within the section; do not merge entries across sections. For html-conversation/2, evidence is the persistent HTML section, including its heading and owned interactive states; labels store its section_id without quotations or offsets. Source capture assigns section IDs once, before exploration, and English translations inherit them. Earlier formats retain their canonical source evidence. Speaker labels and capture metadata are excluded.

Existing annotations use their pinned historical rubric. See coding-guidebook-legacy.md for the retired descriptive-label guide. Rating tasks continue to use rating-questions.md unchanged.

## Persistent section references

Portal sections have permanent IDs within their conversation: `t1s1` is the first section of the first tutor turn, `t1s2` its next section, and `t2s1` the first section of the second tutor turn. Register boundaries once and reuse them across annotators, item labels, LLM baselines, and exports. Resolve an ID using `(conversation_id, section_id)`; an ID alone is not globally unique. The section registry stores canonical turn IDs, UTF-16 source offsets, exact quotations, and a source fingerprint. Labels store `section_id`, item, category, and count; section evidence is resolved when reading. Existing IDs must never be renumbered or assigned new text. Revised conversation content needs a new conversation record.

## Image-only LLM counts

The local image-only pipeline uses Gemini Argon to inspect one rendered tutor turn at a time, with permanent section IDs and names marked on the images. Injected section captions and boundaries are metadata, not instructional features. Every section receives all 15 feature counts (0-1000); only positive counts become saved annotations. Counts include repeated occurrences within the section.

Each positive LLM label includes optional top-level `llm_evidence`: one nonempty line, up to 1000 characters, explaining the visible occurrences supporting its quantity. This is model justification, separate from canonical section source evidence. It is preserved in baseline review and exports; human annotations do not require it. A still image alone cannot establish animation or interaction. Human assignments and ratings are unchanged.

## Hidden question and image quantities

Include hidden or off-screen questions and instructional images when visible counters, numbered pagination, thumbnails, or other explicit quantity cues establish their number. For example, a knowledge-checking quiz showing "Question 1 of 5" counts as 5 questions, and an instructional image carousel showing "Image 2 of 4" contains 4 images, not 5. Count the indicated total once per component within a section, including its visible item; do not add it again for repeated tiles or states of that same component in the section. A Next arrow alone does not establish a quantity: count only the evidenced number. Attribute counts to their own registered section; do not merge sections. For hidden images, use the specific image type only when supported; use Other Images for instructional images whose type cannot be established. Do not also count those images under a specific type. Evidence must cite the quantity cue and distinguish visible from inferred hidden occurrences.

## Interactive state ownership

Captured interaction states are supporting views of the original containing section, not additional sections or additional components. A quiz with three questions remains three questions when the learner selects an answer, views feedback, or navigates Previous/Next. Count each question/image in that component once across its supporting views. Separate components in the same section remain separate; do not deduplicate merely by matching text. Supporting-view captions and capture metadata do not count as headings. Visible section IDs remain permanent. Additional views are stored under their owning section in `conversation_section_states`; historical snapshot section IDs remain readable aliases for archived annotations and are excluded from new labeling.

### Image-focused capture scope

Packages marked `image-focused/1` preserve all discoverable instructional image occurrences and initial views of text-only widgets. Completeness applies to this scope, not exhaustive quiz questions or feedback. Section IDs remain frozen. Gallery views retain their owning component and section; occurrence IDs identify repeated views of the same image. Distinct occurrences may share identical asset bytes. Count each occurrence once across views, while retaining distinct occurrences even when their pixels match. Supporting-view metadata and viewer controls are not instructional headings or interactive media. Explicit quiz counters may support quantities even when later question views were intentionally not explored.
